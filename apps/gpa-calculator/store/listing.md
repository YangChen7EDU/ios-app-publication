## Description (4000)

Know exactly where your GPA stands.

GPA Calculator helps high school students track their grades term by term and see both their weighted and unweighted GPA at a glance.

ORGANIZED BY TERM
Add each term, like "9th Grade · Fall", and the courses you took. See each term's GPA and your cumulative GPA across all of high school.

WEIGHTED AND UNWEIGHTED
Mark a course as Regular, Honors or AP / IB. Honors adds 0.5 and AP / IB adds 1.0 to your weighted GPA, the way most US high schools calculate it. Your unweighted GPA is shown right next to it.

CREDITS THAT COUNT
Year-long and semester classes are weighted by their credits, so a half-credit elective doesn't count as much as a full-year class.

FITS YOUR SCHOOL
Choose whether an A+ counts as 4.0 or 4.3.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your grades stay on your device.

## App Review notes

> GPA Calculator is a local-only utility for high school students. No sign-in, no network access, no data collection. To try it: tap Add Term, then + to add courses with a grade, level and credits. The term and cumulative GPA update immediately. Settings → Delete All Data removes everything.
