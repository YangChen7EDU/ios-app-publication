# GPA Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

GPA Calculator does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **What the app stores**: the terms, courses, grades and settings you enter. They are saved only on your device.
- **What is sent off your device**: nothing. The app makes no network requests and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
- **Third parties**: none receive your data.
- **Children**: the app collects no data from anyone, including children.
- **Deleting your data**: use Settings → Delete All Data in the app, or delete the app.

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/YangChen7EDU/ios-app-publication/issues.
